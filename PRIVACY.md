# Super Exporter Privacy Policy

Effective 5 November 2026. This policy applies to Super Exporter v1.1.4.

## Data handled and purpose

When you open the export panel on a supported AI chat website, Super Exporter reads visible conversation content and displays message previews. When you request an export, it processes selected messages, including available text, images, links and file references, to prepare your file. An export can include the conversation title and source URL. You can disable inclusion of the source URL in settings.

## Local processing and retention

Conversation content is processed in the browser and is not sent to servers operated by the developer. The extension does not use analytics or advertising. It stores your export preferences in Chrome's local extension storage. Exported files remain where you save them until you delete them. Removing the extension removes its local settings. You can delete exported files separately.

## Image requests

To include an image, the extension can request it from the chat website. The browser may use your existing session for a same-origin request. External image requests are disabled by default. If you enable them, the extension can request images from external HTTPS hosts without sending cookies or a referrer. The image host receives the requested URL and normal network information such as your IP address. The extension does not upload chat text as part of these requests.

## Sharing and use limits

Super Exporter does not sell user data or use it for advertising, credit scoring or lending. It uses conversation content to provide the requested export and handles user data in accordance with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You control where you save or share the files you export.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the publisher through the support contact listed on the extension's Chrome Web Store page or the [project's support page](https://github.com/huyuxuan-peter/super-exporter/issues). Please do not include private conversation content in public issues.
